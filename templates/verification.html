Why operator equivalence works

A unitary circuit has a matrix describing its effect on every possible quantum input. Comparing those matrices checks the complete operation, rather than just a few sample inputs.

U_original = e^(iφ) × U_transpiled

A global phase multiplies the entire state by the same complex phase and does not change observable measurement probabilities. Gate counts and depths can differ while the quantum operation stays the same.

This is a numerical matrix comparison within stated tolerances, not a symbolic proof. Measured circuits use the separate method shown above, not a unitary matrix for the complete circuit.
